Classic Tangy Lemon Bars

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 35 mins Servings: 16 squares Category: Dessert

A good lemon bar is bracing rather than sweet: a thin shortbread base, a lemon filling that makes you squint slightly, and a dusting of powdered sugar on top. This version uses the juice of two lemons and a small amount of flour in the filling so it sets into a clean, sliceable layer.

The recipe makes sixteen squares from an eight-inch pan and takes about fifty minutes including baking and cooling. They keep well, which makes them useful for a bake sale or a lunchbox.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • Juice of 2 large lemons (about 1/3 cup)
  • Extra powdered sugar for dusting

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease an 8x8-inch square baking dish.
  2. Make the crust: In a medium bowl, blend flour, powdered sugar, softened butter, and a pinch of salt together until it forms a cohesive dough. Press firmly into a flat layer along the bottom of your baking pan. Bake for 15 minutes until set.
  3. Make the filling: While the crust bakes, whisk together granulated sugar and 2 tablespoons of flour. Whisk in the eggs and fresh lemon juice until smooth and frothy.
  4. Pour the lemon filling carefully over the hot baked shortbread crust.
  5. Return to the oven and bake for 20 minutes until the filling is fully set. Let cool completely, dust generously with powdered sugar, and slice into clean squares.

Why this recipe works

The shortbread base is pressed into the pan and baked before the filling goes on, so it stays crisp under the custard. Cooking the filling just until it sets but still has a slight wobble, then cooling it completely in the fridge, gives the clean texture that a flour-thickened lemon filling is known for.

Tips for success

  • Press the shortbread into an even layer and chill it for ten minutes before baking, so it does not shrink.
  • Whisk the filling thoroughly but do not whip air into it, or the surface will bubble.
  • Dust with powdered sugar just before serving, since it dissolves into the surface after a few hours.

Substitutions that work

  • Limes or Meyer lemons work well in place of regular lemons, with a slightly different acidity.
  • Add a teaspoon of lemon zest to the shortbread for a stronger citrus note.
  • For a gluten-free base, use a gluten-free flour blend and add a tablespoon of cornstarch.

Storage and make-ahead

Keeps in the refrigerator, covered, for four days, or freeze cut squares for two months. Serve them cold or at cool room temperature. The base softens slowly over time, so they are best in the first two days.

Serving suggestions

Serve as a dessert with coffee, or as an afternoon treat with a glass of iced tea. A few raspberries alongside cut the sweetness nicely.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my lemon filling runny?

It was underbaked or the eggs were not whisked in fully. Bake until the filling is set at the edges and only slightly loose in the middle, then chill for at least two hours.

Can I use bottled lemon juice?

Fresh juice gives a much better flavour, but bottled works in a pinch. Add a little zest to make up for the missing oils.

How do I get clean cuts?

Chill the bars thoroughly, wipe the knife between cuts and dip it in hot water before slicing.
