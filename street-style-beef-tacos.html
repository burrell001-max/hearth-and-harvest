Street-Style Beef Tacos

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 15 mins Servings: 4 Category: Quick Meals

Illustrative image.

Street-style tacos are about the tortilla and the toppings as much as the meat. Seasoned beef goes into small corn tortillas that have been warmed until they smell of toasted corn, then it is finished with raw white onion, cilantro and a squeeze of lime.

There is no cheese, no lettuce and no sour cream, and the tacos are better for it. Twelve small tortillas and a pound of beef serve four in about thirty minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef (or flank steak, finely diced)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 small white onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 lime wedges for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and cook until entirely browned, breaking it apart with a spoon as it cooks (about 6-8 minutes). Drain any excess grease.
  2. Turn the heat down to low. Add the chili powder, cumin, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Splash in 2-3 tablespoons of water to help distribute the spices. Stir well and simmer for 2 minutes until flavorful, then remove from heat.
  3. In a clean, dry skillet over medium-high heat, warm the corn tortillas for about 30 seconds on each side until pliable and slightly charred. Wrap them in a clean kitchen towel to keep warm.
  4. Assemble the tacos by stacking two warm corn tortillas per taco (the street-style way to prevent tearing). Scoop a generous spoonful of seasoned beef into the center.
  5. Top with a handful of finely diced white onion and fresh chopped cilantro. Serve warm with lime wedges on the side for squeezing over the top!

Why this recipe works

Warming the tortillas directly in a dry pan, rather than steaming them in a packet, gives them a slight char and keeps them from tearing. The raw onion and cilantro cut through the richness of the beef, and lime at the table keeps everything sharp.

Tips for success

  • Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet for about thirty seconds a side, then stack them in a clean towel to stay soft.
  • Break the beef up finely as it cooks so it fills the small tortillas evenly.
  • Dice the onion very small. Big pieces overpower the taco.

Substitutions that work

  • Flank steak, finely diced, can replace the ground beef and gives a chewier, more traditional texture.
  • Flour tortillas work but are softer and less sturdy; use the small fajita size.
  • Add pickled jalapenos, radish slices or crumbled queso fresco for a different topping set.

Storage and make-ahead

Refrigerate the cooked beef for three days and reheat in a skillet until hot. Keep the toppings separate and chop fresh onion and cilantro at serving time. Warm tortillas are best fresh, though they can be wrapped and reheated.

Serving suggestions

Serve with lime wedges, a bowl of salsa and a side of black beans or Mexican rice. A cold beer or a glass of horchata is the right drink.

Frequently asked questions

What makes tacos street-style?

Small corn tortillas, simple seasoned meat and fresh toppings such as onion, cilantro and lime, rather than cheese, lettuce and sour cream.

Can I use flour tortillas?

Yes, though corn tortillas are traditional and give the toasted flavour that defines this style. Use the small size either way.

How do I stop corn tortillas cracking?

Warm them in a dry pan or directly over a flame until pliable, and keep them wrapped in a towel so they stay soft while you fill them.
