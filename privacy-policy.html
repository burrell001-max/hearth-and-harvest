Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you visit Hearth & Harvest at hearth-and-harvest.xyz. It covers the site itself, the advertising served by Google AdSense, and the analytics used to understand traffic.

Who is responsible

Hearth & Harvest is an independent recipe website published by Kent B Williams. Questions about this policy, or requests about your information, can be sent to hearth-and-harvest@outlook.com.

Information the site collects directly

The site does not have accounts, sign-in, comment forms or newsletter sign-ups. Nothing you type into the search box is transmitted anywhere; the search filters the recipes that are already loaded in your browser. The site does not ask for your name, address or payment details, and it does not build a profile of you.

Like any website, the hosting provider records standard technical information when a page is requested, including the page address, the time of the request, the browser and operating system reported by your device, and an approximate location derived from the network. These server logs are used for security and to keep the site working.

Cookies and local storage

Hearth & Harvest does not set its own cookies and does not write to your browser's local storage. Cookies you encounter on this site come from Google's advertising and analytics services, described below. The cookie policy explains each one and how to control them.

Advertising: Google AdSense

This site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies, including the advertising cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. This is what allows Google to show advertising that reflects your interests rather than repeating the same ad on every page.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. Third-party vendors and ad networks may also place or read cookies on your browser through the ad units on this site. The site owner does not receive or control the data those cookies contain, and does not choose which individual ads are shown.

You can opt out of personalised advertising, and review or change the ad settings associated with your Google account, at Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of personalised advertising from many third-party vendors at aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Opting out of personalised advertising does not mean you will see no ads; it means the ads are less closely matched to your interests.

Analytics: Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which pages are read and how visitors arrive. Google Analytics collects information such as pages viewed, time on page, approximate location, browser and device type, and may use cookies or similar identifiers to recognise returning sessions. Reports available to the site owner are aggregated; individual visitors are not identified by name.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can prevent your data being used by Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking analytics cookies in your browser settings.

Consent for visitors in Europe and the UK

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a Google consent message before advertising or analytics cookies are used for personalised purposes. Until you make a choice, the site signals a default of denied for advertising storage, advertising user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage, in line with Google Consent Mode. Your choice can be changed at any time through the consent message, which can be reopened from the link on the page where it appears, or by clearing the site's cookies and reloading.

Privacy choices for visitors in the United States

Visitors in states with applicable privacy laws, such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others, can manage their advertising privacy choices through Google's privacy message shown on this site, and through the links to Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info above. Those messages allow you to decline personalised advertising and, where the law provides for it, to exercise rights over the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising purposes.

Requests and deletion

Because the site does not hold accounts or contact lists, there is usually no personal record to delete on this site. If you have emailed the site address, that message can be deleted at your request: write to hearth-and-harvest@outlook.com with the subject line "data request" and describe what you would like removed. Requests are handled within 30 days.

For data held by Google through advertising or analytics, use the Google Privacy Policy and the tools linked above, since Google controls that data rather than this site.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to external services, for example Google's policy pages. This policy does not cover those sites, and their own privacy policies apply once you leave this one.

Changes to this policy

This policy is reviewed when the site changes, for example if a new service is added. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised, and the current version always applies.
