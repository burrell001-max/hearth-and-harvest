Easy Chicken Quesadillas

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Total: 20 mins Servings: 4 quesadillas

These chicken quesadillas are crisp on the outside, melty on the inside and ready in about 20 minutes. Rotisserie chicken makes them fast, and a little salsa and spice keep the filling flavorful and moist.

They are easy to customize with whatever vegetables or cheese you have on hand.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 4 large (10-inch) flour tortillas
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Monterey Jack or Mexican blend cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil, for the pan
  • Chopped cilantro, sour cream and extra salsa, for serving

Instructions

  1. Mix the chicken with the salsa, cumin and chili powder.
  2. Sprinkle cheese over one half of each tortilla, top with a quarter of the chicken, then more cheese if you like. Fold the tortilla over.
  3. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add a little butter. Cook each quesadilla for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese has melted.
  4. Rest for a minute, cut into wedges and serve with sour cream, salsa and cilantro.

Ingredient Notes and Substitutions

Cheese

Shred your own for the best melt. Pepper Jack adds heat.

Tortillas

Large flour tortillas fold best. Warm them briefly if they crack.

Tips for Success

  • Do not overfill, or the filling will spill out.
  • Keep the heat at medium so the cheese melts before the tortilla burns.
  • Drain very wet salsa before mixing.

Variations

  • Add black beans, corn or sautéed peppers.
  • Swap the chicken for sautéed mushrooms or beans.

Storage and Make-Ahead

Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days and reheat in a skillet or oven at 375°F until crisp. They can be frozen for up to 2 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep them crispy?

Cook in a dry or lightly buttered skillet, and do not stack them while hot.

Can I bake them?

Yes. Bake on a sheet at 425°F for about 10 minutes, flipping once.

What if my chicken is plain?

The salsa and spices season it, but add a pinch of salt if needed.
