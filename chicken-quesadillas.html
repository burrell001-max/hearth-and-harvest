Easy Chicken Quesadillas

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Servings: 4 quesadillas Category: Quick Meals

Illustrative image.

Quesadillas are the answer to a fridge with leftover chicken and a short amount of patience. A flour tortilla, a layer of seasoned chicken and enough cheese to bind it all together is really all you need, and the whole thing cooks in about four minutes a side.

The version below seasons the chicken with cumin and chili powder and mixes in salsa, which keeps the filling moist without making the tortilla soggy. Four quesadillas feed four people, or two very hungry ones.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 4 large (10-inch) flour tortillas
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Monterey Jack or Mexican blend cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil, for the pan
  • Chopped cilantro, sour cream and extra salsa, for serving

Instructions

  1. Mix the chicken with the salsa, cumin and chili powder.
  2. Sprinkle cheese over one half of each tortilla, top with a quarter of the chicken, then more cheese if you like. Fold the tortilla over.
  3. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add a little butter. Cook each quesadilla for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese has melted.
  4. Rest for a minute, cut into wedges and serve with sour cream, salsa and cilantro.
  5. Do not overfill, or the filling will spill out.
  6. Keep the heat at medium so the cheese melts before the tortilla burns.
  7. Drain very wet salsa before mixing.
  8. Add black beans, corn or sautéed peppers.
  9. Swap the chicken for sautéed mushrooms or beans.

Why this recipe works

Cheese on both sides of the filling acts as glue, so the quesadilla holds together when you flip it and cut it. Medium heat melts the cheese fully before the tortilla darkens, which is the difference between a crisp golden shell and a burnt one with a cold centre.

Tips for success

  • Grate the cheese yourself. Pre-shredded cheese is coated with starch and melts less smoothly.
  • Drain any watery salsa before mixing it into the chicken, or the tortilla will steam instead of crisp.
  • Press the quesadilla gently with a spatula as it cooks so the layers make contact and the cheese seals.

Substitutions that work

  • Monterey Jack, cheddar, Oaxaca or a Mexican blend all melt well. Use whatever you have.
  • Black beans mashed lightly with the chicken stretch the filling and add substance without more meat.
  • Corn tortillas work for a gluten-free version, though they crack more easily and are better cooked one at a time.

Storage and make-ahead

Refrigerate leftover filling for three days and assemble fresh quesadillas when you want them. Cooked quesadillas keep for two days and reheat best in a dry skillet for a minute a side, which restores the crispness that a microwave destroys.

Serving suggestions

Serve with sour cream, extra salsa, sliced avocado and a squeeze of lime. A simple tomato salad or a bowl of black beans turns it into a fuller meal.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make quesadillas without cheese?

Cheese is what holds the filling together, so leaving it out makes the quesadilla fall apart. Use a smaller amount of a strongly flavoured cheese instead.

Why are my quesadillas soggy?

Wet fillings and low heat are the usual causes. Drain the salsa, do not overfill, and cook over medium heat until the tortilla is crisp and golden.

Can I bake quesadillas instead of frying them?

Yes. Brush them with oil and bake at 425 F for about ten minutes, turning once, though the edges will be crisper and less evenly golden.
