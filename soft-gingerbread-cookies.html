Soft Gingerbread Cookies

Prep Time: 30 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Total: 40 mins Servings: about 24 cookies

These gingerbread cookies are soft in the middle with lightly crisp edges and a warm blend of ginger, cinnamon and cloves. The dough rolls and cuts cleanly, so it works for gingerbread people, stars or any cookie cutter you own.

The dough needs at least 2 hours in the refrigerator before rolling, so plan ahead. It can also be made the day before.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tbsp ground ginger
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup unsulphured molasses
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, baking soda, ginger, cinnamon, cloves and salt in a bowl.
  2. In a large bowl, beat the butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Beat in the egg, molasses and vanilla.
  3. Add the dry ingredients and mix on low until a soft dough forms. Divide in half, flatten into disks, wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  4. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line baking sheets with parchment.
  5. Roll one disk on a lightly floured surface to about 1/4 inch thick. Cut out shapes and place them 1 inch apart on the sheets.
  6. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the edges look set. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes, then move to a wire rack. Decorate once completely cool.

Ingredient Notes and Substitutions

Molasses

Use regular unsulphured molasses. Blackstrap is very bitter and will overpower the cookies.

Butter

Softened means it dents easily when pressed but is not greasy or melting.

Spices

Fresh spices matter. If your ground ginger has been in the cupboard for years, replace it.

Tips for Success

  • Chill the dough thoroughly. Warm dough spreads and loses its shape.
  • Roll the dough evenly so the cookies bake at the same rate.
  • For softer cookies, bake closer to 8 minutes. Bake longer for a crisper cookie.
  • If the dough gets sticky while rolling, return it to the refrigerator for 10 minutes.

Variations

  • Decorate with royal icing, or simple icing made from powdered sugar and milk.
  • Roll the dough into balls, dip in sugar and bake for chewy gingerbread cookies instead of cut-outs.
  • Add 1 tsp orange zest for a citrusy note.

Storage and Make-Ahead

Store decorated or plain cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days. The dough keeps in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, or you can freeze wrapped disks for up to 3 months and thaw overnight in the refrigerator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my gingerbread cookies spread?

The dough was probably too warm or the butter too soft. Chill the dough well and keep the unrolled portion in the refrigerator.

Can I skip the chilling?

I would not. The dough is too soft to roll without chilling and the cookies will lose their shape.

How do I make them crisper?

Roll thinner, to about 1/8 inch, and bake a minute or two longer.

How many cookies does this make?

About 24 cookies with a 3-inch cutter. The count varies with the size of the cutter.
