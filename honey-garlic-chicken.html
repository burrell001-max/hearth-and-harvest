Honey Garlic Glazed Chicken

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 15 mins Servings: 4 Category: Dinner

Honey garlic chicken is a weeknight staple for good reason. Chicken breasts are dredged lightly in flour, seared until golden, then finished in a sticky sauce of honey, soy, garlic and a splash of vinegar that reduces to a glaze in the pan.

It takes about thirty minutes and uses one skillet. Four servings, and the sauce is good enough that you will want bread to mop the pan.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (pounded to even thickness)
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour (for dredging)
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • Sliced green onions and sesame seeds for garnish

Instructions

  1. Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper, then lightly coat each side in flour, shaking off the excess.
  2. Heat olive oil and 1 tablespoon of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken for 5-6 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through. Remove chicken to a plate.
  3. Reduce skillet heat to medium. Melt the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter, then add minced garlic. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the honey, soy sauce, and apple cider vinegar. Stir well and let the sauce simmer for 2-3 minutes until it begins to thicken into a glossy glaze.
  5. Return the chicken to the skillet, spooning the rich honey garlic sauce over the top until beautifully coated. Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.

Why this recipe works

A thin coat of flour gives the chicken a surface the glaze can cling to and browns faster than a bare breast. Adding the honey and soy after the chicken is seared, rather than before, keeps the sugars from scorching while the meat cooks through.

Tips for success

  • Pound the chicken to an even thickness so it cooks through before the outside dries out.
  • Sear in a hot pan and do not move the chicken for the first three minutes, so a crust forms.
  • Turn the heat down to low before adding the honey mixture, and spoon it over the chicken as it thickens.

Substitutions that work

  • Chicken thighs work better than breasts if you tend to overcook them; add five minutes to the cooking time.
  • Tamari or coconut aminos can replace the soy sauce for a gluten-free version.
  • Rice vinegar or lemon juice can stand in for the apple cider vinegar.

Storage and make-ahead

Refrigerate leftovers for three days in a covered container, with a spoonful of water to keep the glaze from setting rock hard. Reheat gently in a pan, not the microwave, so the sauce loosens. It does not freeze well once glazed.

Serving suggestions

Serve over steamed rice, sesame noodles or mashed potatoes, with steamed broccoli or green beans on the side. Scatter green onions and sesame seeds over the top.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my honey garlic sauce burn?

The heat was too high. Add the honey mixture only after the chicken is seared, and finish over low heat, stirring so the sugars do not catch.

Can I make this in the oven?

Yes. Sear the chicken on the stove, add the sauce, and finish in a 375 F oven for fifteen minutes, spooning the glaze over halfway through.

How do I make the sauce thicker?

Let it reduce over low heat until it coats the back of a spoon, or stir in a small cornstarch slurry and simmer for a minute.
