Contact Hearth & Harvest

Questions about a recipe, a correction, or something that did not turn out the way the page described? Email is the only contact channel, and every message is read.

Email

hearth-and-harvest@outlook.com

Replies usually take a few business days. The site is run by Kent B Williams alongside other work, so a message sent on a Friday may not be answered until the middle of the following week. If your question is about a specific recipe, put the recipe name in the subject line.

What to include

For a recipe question, the recipe name and the step you are stuck on are enough. For a correction, it helps to know what the page says, what happened when you cooked it, and roughly what equipment you used, since pan size and oven calibration explain a lot of differences. For a substitution question, mention what you have in the cupboard and what you are trying to avoid, and you will get a more useful answer than a simple yes or no.

What the site can and cannot help with

Hearth & Harvest can answer questions about the recipes published here, correct errors on its own pages, and discuss advertising or licensing enquiries. It cannot give medical, legal or financial advice, and it cannot take responsibility for how a recipe turns out in your kitchen, since ingredients, equipment and oven temperatures vary.

Advertising and permissions

For advertising, sponsorship or permission to reproduce a recipe or photograph, use the same address and say what you have in mind. Recipes and photographs on this site may not be republished without permission; the terms of use explain what is and is not allowed.

Privacy

Email sent to the address above is used only to reply to you. It is not added to a mailing list, sold or shared, and it can be deleted on request. The privacy policy explains how to ask for that, and what the site's advertising and analytics partners collect when you browse.
