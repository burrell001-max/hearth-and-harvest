Classic Homemade Lasagna

Prep Time: 30 mins Cook Time: 50 mins Servings: 8 Category: Dinner

Illustrative image.

Lasagna is a project, but it is a rewarding one. A slow-simmered meat sauce, a ricotta layer lightened with egg, and enough mozzarella to hold everything together bake into something far greater than the sum of its parts.

This version uses twelve noodles and a 9x13 inch dish, serving eight. It is worth making the full batch because it reheats beautifully and freezes well in portions.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles (cooked according to package instructions)
  • 1 lb lean ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg (beaten)
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tsp dried Italian seasoning
  • Salt, pepper, and fresh parsley to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown the ground beef (or sausage) with the chopped onion and minced garlic until the meat is fully cooked. Drain off excess grease.
  2. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, a pinch of salt, and black pepper. Lower the heat and let the meat sauce simmer gently for 15-20 minutes.
  3. In a medium mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, the beaten egg, 1/2 cup of the shredded mozzarella, the grated Parmesan cheese, and a tablespoon of chopped fresh parsley. Mix well.
  4. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Spray a 9x13-inch baking dish with non-stick cooking spray.
  5. **Assemble the layers:** Spread 1 cup of meat sauce across the bottom of the dish. Arrange 4 lasagna noodles over the sauce. Spread half of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles, then top with 1 cup of meat sauce and 1 cup of mozzarella. Repeat the layers (noodles, ricotta, sauce, mozzarella) one more time. Top with final noodles, remaining sauce, and the rest of the mozzarella cheese.
  6. Cover the dish loosely with aluminum foil (so it doesn't stick to the cheese) and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for an additional 20-25 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  7. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing and serving so the layers stay beautifully intact.

Why this recipe works

Simmering the meat sauce with tomato paste gives it a concentrated flavour that a quick sauce lacks, and letting it cool slightly before layering keeps the noodles from sliding. The ricotta mixture needs the egg to set so the layers hold when you cut into the dish.

Tips for success

  • Cook the noodles a minute short of al dente and lay them on a towel so they do not stick together.
  • Spread a thin layer of sauce on the bottom of the dish before the first noodles, so nothing sticks.
  • Let the lasagna rest for fifteen minutes after baking before cutting, or the layers will slide apart.

Substitutions that work

  • No-boil noodles work well; add an extra half cup of sauce and bake covered for the first thirty minutes.
  • Cottage cheese drained in a sieve can replace the ricotta for a lighter filling.
  • For a vegetarian version, replace the meat with sautéed mushrooms, zucchini and a cup of lentils.

Storage and make-ahead

Refrigerate covered for four days. Reheat portions in the microwave or the oven at 350 F covered for twenty minutes. Assemble unbaked up to two days ahead, or freeze unbaked for three months and bake from frozen with an extra thirty minutes.

Serving suggestions

Serve with garlic bread and a simple green salad dressed with olive oil and lemon. A glass of red wine and a nap afterwards are traditional.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to cook the lasagna noodles first?

Regular noodles need boiling. No-boil noodles can go straight into the dish, but they need more sauce so they hydrate properly.

Why did my lasagna turn out watery?

The sauce was too thin or the ricotta was not drained. Simmer the sauce until it is thick and drain the ricotta for ten minutes before mixing it.

Can I freeze lasagna?

Yes, and it is best frozen unbaked. Wrap it well, freeze for up to three months, then bake from frozen at 350 F for about ninety minutes, covered for most of the time.
