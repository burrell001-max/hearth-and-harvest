Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on Hearth & Harvest, what each one is for, and how you can control them. It should be read alongside the privacy policy, which covers the wider question of what information is collected.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. It can remember a preference, keep a session open, or help a service recognise a browser across visits. Related technologies, such as local storage and pixels, work in similar ways, and this policy covers them together.

Cookies set by this site

Hearth & Harvest does not set cookies of its own, does not use local storage and does not track you between pages. There are no accounts, no shopping basket and no preference switches that need remembering. Every recipe page is a plain document that can be read without any script running at all.

Google AdSense

Advertising on this site is served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to measure how ads perform. These cookies may identify your browser to Google, though not by name to this site.

You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, opt out of personalised ads from many vendors at aboutads.info, and read how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 records aggregate information about visits: which pages are read, how long people stay, what kind of device and browser they use, and roughly where they are. It uses cookies and similar identifiers to distinguish sessions. The reports the site owner sees do not identify individual visitors. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a Google consent message appears before advertising and analytics cookies are used for personalised purposes. The site sets a default of denied for advertising storage, advertising user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage until you make a choice. Declining means you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised using those cookies. You can change your choice by reopening the consent message or by clearing this site's cookies in your browser and reloading the page.

Choices in the United States

Visitors in states with applicable privacy laws can use Google's privacy message on this site to manage advertising privacy choices, alongside the Ads Settings and aboutads.info links above.

How to control cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you see, block and delete cookies. The settings pages below explain how:

  • Chrome: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies.
  • Safari: Settings, then Privacy, then Manage Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Cookies and Site Data.
  • Edge: Settings, then Cookies and site permissions, then Manage and delete cookies.

Blocking all cookies will stop personalised advertising and analytics, and the recipes will continue to work normally. Blocking cookies only for this site is also possible in most browsers, and is a reasonable middle ground if you want the recipes without the advertising identifiers. Deleting cookies resets any consent choice you have made, so the consent message may appear again on your next visit.

Changes to this policy

If the site adds a service that uses cookies, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. The contact page is the place to ask questions about it.
