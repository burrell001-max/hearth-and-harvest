# Media credits

Every image on hearth-and-harvest.xyz is there to show the dish a recipe produces or to head an article. Images are illustrative: they show the shape, colour and texture the method is aiming for, not a photograph of the exact pan a reader will make, and results vary with ingredients, pans and ovens.

Recipe and article images are served from `assets/images/recipes/` as WebP files, cropped to 1200×800 and compressed below 180 KB. Each image carries descriptive alt text on the page.

Nothing on this site uses an image of unrecorded origin. Each file below is either an illustration made for Hearth & Harvest or a photograph used under the Unsplash License, with the identifier recorded.

### Redrawn after review (3)

Three first attempts did not show what the page said they showed, and were replaced:

- `banana-bread` — the first version was studded with chocolate chips, which the recipe does not use.
- `classic-sage-stuffing` — the first version had a melted-cheese crust; the recipe has no cheese.
- `garlic-parmesan-wings` — the first version looked like stuffed chicken rolls rather than bone-in wings.

## How the images were checked

Every recipe image was opened and compared against the alt text on its page, looking for dishes, ingredients or serving vessels that the recipe does not produce. That review found the three mismatches listed above, which were redrawn.

One alt text was corrected rather than the image: `egg-fried-rice` shows a soft omelette folded over the rice, not egg scrambled through it, so its alt text now describes that instead of claiming otherwise.
