Classic Pumpkin Pie

Prep Time: 20 mins Cook Time: 65 mins Total: 85 mins Servings: 8

No Thanksgiving table feels complete without a pumpkin pie. This classic version uses canned pumpkin and evaporated milk to make a silky, lightly spiced custard that sets perfectly in a flaky crust.

It is straightforward to make, and it actually benefits from being made the day before, since the pie needs time to cool and chill fully.

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch deep-dish pie crust
  • 1 can (15 oz) pure pumpkin puree (not pumpkin pie filling)
  • 1 can (12 fl oz) evaporated milk
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Whipped cream, for serving

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C) and place the unbaked crust in a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the sugar, cinnamon, ginger, cloves and salt. Add the eggs and beat lightly.
  3. Stir in the pumpkin puree, then gradually whisk in the evaporated milk until smooth.
  4. Pour the filling into the crust. Bake for 15 minutes at 425°F.
  5. Reduce the oven to 350°F (175°C) and bake for 40 to 50 minutes more, until a knife inserted near the center comes out clean. The center may still jiggle slightly.
  6. Cool the pie on a wire rack for at least 2 hours, then refrigerate until serving. Serve with whipped cream.

Ingredient Notes and Substitutions

Pumpkin puree

Check the label. Pure pumpkin puree is just pumpkin. Pumpkin pie filling already contains sugar and spices and will throw off the recipe.

Evaporated milk

It gives the custard a rich, creamy texture. Do not use sweetened condensed milk.

Spices

You can replace the three spices with 2 teaspoons of pumpkin pie spice.

The crust

A refrigerated crust works well. Homemade is a nice upgrade if you have time.

Tips for Success

  • Do not overbake. A slightly wobbly center sets as it cools, and overbaking causes cracks.
  • If the crust edge browns too fast, cover it loosely with foil or a pie shield.
  • Cool the pie slowly at room temperature before chilling to help prevent cracking.
  • Place the pie plate on a baking sheet to catch drips and make it easier to move.

Variations

  • Top with a pecan streusel or maple whipped cream.
  • Use a gingersnap cookie crust for extra spice.
  • Bake in individual ramekins without a crust for a crustless pumpkin custard.

Storage and Make-Ahead

Because it is an egg and dairy custard, store the pie in the refrigerator once it has cooled, and do not leave it out for more than 2 hours. Cover and keep it for up to 4 days. The pie can be baked a day or two ahead. You can freeze it for up to a month, thawing it overnight in the refrigerator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my pumpkin pie crack?

Usually from overbaking or cooling too quickly. Pull it out when the center still jiggles slightly and let it cool gradually.

How do I know when it is done?

A knife inserted about an inch from the center should come out clean.

Can I use fresh pumpkin?

Yes, but it must be roasted, pureed and well drained, since fresh puree is wetter than canned.

Should pumpkin pie be refrigerated?

Yes. Because it contains eggs and milk, refrigerate it once it has cooled.
