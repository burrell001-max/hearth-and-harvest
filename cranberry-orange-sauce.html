Cranberry Orange Sauce

Prep Time: 5 mins Cook Time: 15 mins Total: 20 mins Servings: 8

Homemade cranberry sauce is one of the easiest things on the holiday table, and it tastes far brighter than anything from a can. Fresh orange juice and zest balance the tartness of the berries, and a pinch of cinnamon adds a little warmth.

It takes about 20 minutes on the stove and is best made ahead, which frees up your oven and your attention on the big day.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz (about 3 cups) fresh or frozen cranberries
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup fresh orange juice
  • 1/4 cup water
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 1 cinnamon stick (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Combine the cranberries, sugar, orange juice, water, zest, cinnamon stick and salt in a medium saucepan.
  2. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally, then reduce to a steady simmer.
  3. Cook for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until most of the berries have burst and the sauce looks glossy.
  4. Remove from the heat and discard the cinnamon stick. The sauce will look thin but thickens a lot as it cools.
  5. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until ready to serve.

Ingredient Notes and Substitutions

Fresh or frozen cranberries

Both work. Use frozen berries straight from the freezer and add a minute or two to the cooking time.

Sugar

3/4 cup gives a tart, balanced sauce. Add up to 1/4 cup more if you prefer it sweeter. Maple syrup or honey can replace part of the sugar.

Orange

Zest the orange before juicing it. Bottled juice works if you are short on time, but fresh tastes brighter.

Tips for Success

  • Do not overcook it. The pectin in cranberries sets the sauce as it cools.
  • Stir in a splash of water if it is too thick after chilling.
  • Taste once it is cool, since it will taste sweeter warm.
  • A spoonful of orange liqueur or a squeeze of lemon at the end adds depth.

Variations

  • Add 1/2 cup chopped pecans or walnuts just before serving.
  • Stir in a teaspoon of fresh grated ginger while cooking.
  • Replace half the water with apple cider for a deeper, fruitier flavor.

Storage and Make-Ahead

Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 1 week. It can be made up to 3 days ahead, and the flavor improves overnight. For longer storage, freeze it for up to 3 months and thaw in the refrigerator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my cranberry sauce runny?

It thickens as it cools. If it is still thin after chilling, simmer it for a few more minutes.

Can I make it without refined sugar?

Maple syrup or honey can replace the sugar, but the texture and sweetness will differ slightly, so adjust to taste.

What do I serve it with?

Turkey, roast chicken, ham and stuffing, and it also works on sandwiches or spooned over cream cheese with crackers.

Why is it bitter?

Overcooking, or too much orange pith in the zest. Zest only the colored part of the peel.
