Cooking Notes

Short, practical writing about the things that make cooking easier: what to keep in the cupboard, how to cut an onion without swearing, how long leftovers actually last, and which substitutions work when you are halfway through a recipe and missing something.

These are not recipe pages. Each article takes one subject and goes through it properly, with the reasoning behind the advice so you can adapt it rather than follow it blindly. Where a technique belongs with a specific dish, the article links to the recipe on this site that uses it.
