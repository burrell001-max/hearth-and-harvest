Caprese Grilled Cheese

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Total: 20 mins Servings: 4 sandwiches

This caprese grilled cheese takes the flavors of the classic salad and puts them between two slices of crisp, buttery bread. Fresh mozzarella melts beautifully, and basil and tomato add freshness to the gooey cheese.

It is a quick lunch or light dinner, and pairs well with a bowl of tomato soup.

Ingredients

  • 8 slices sourdough or ciabatta
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • 2 ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp basil pesto (optional)
  • 3 tbsp softened butter or olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Balsamic glaze, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Pat the tomato and mozzarella slices dry with paper towels and season the tomatoes lightly with salt and pepper.
  2. Spread pesto on the inside of 4 slices of bread. Layer mozzarella, tomato and basil, then top with the remaining bread.
  3. Butter the outside of each sandwich.
  4. Cook in a skillet over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side, covered, until golden and the cheese is melted.
  5. Drizzle with balsamic glaze if you like, slice and serve.

Ingredient Notes and Substitutions

Mozzarella

Fresh mozzarella has a lot of moisture, so pat it dry. Low-moisture mozzarella also works.

Tomatoes

Use ripe, firm tomatoes such as Roma or beefsteak. Remove the watery seeds if they are very juicy.

Tips for Success

  • Medium-low heat lets the cheese melt before the bread burns.
  • Covering the pan helps the cheese melt.
  • Dry the tomatoes so the bread stays crisp.

Variations

  • Add prosciutto or grilled chicken.
  • Use mayonnaise on the outside of the bread for an extra-crisp crust.

Storage and Make-Ahead

Best eaten right away. Reheat leftovers in a skillet or toaster oven so the bread crisps up again, not in the microwave.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my sandwich soggy?

Wet tomatoes or mozzarella. Pat them dry and salt the tomatoes lightly.

Can I use pesto?

Yes, store-bought or homemade.

What bread is best?

Sturdy bread such as sourdough, ciabatta or a country loaf.
