Easy Peppermint Bark

Prep Time: 20 mins Cook Time: 5 mins Total: 25 mins Servings: about 24 pieces

Peppermint bark is a no-bake holiday classic: a snappy layer of dark chocolate topped with creamy white chocolate and crunchy crushed candy canes. It looks impressive and takes only about 25 minutes of work, plus chilling.

Packaged in tins or bags, it is also a wonderful homemade gift.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz semisweet chocolate, chopped (or chips)
  • 10 oz white chocolate, chopped (or chips)
  • 1/2 tsp peppermint extract
  • 3/4 cup crushed candy canes (about 6 to 8 canes)

Instructions

  1. Line a 9x13-inch baking pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the semisweet chocolate in a microwave-safe bowl in 30-second bursts, stirring each time, until smooth. Spread evenly in the pan and refrigerate for 15 to 20 minutes, until firm.
  3. Melt the white chocolate the same way and stir in the peppermint extract.
  4. Spread the white chocolate over the dark layer and sprinkle immediately with the crushed candy canes, pressing lightly.
  5. Chill for about 1 hour, until completely set. Break into pieces.

Ingredient Notes and Substitutions

Chocolate

Use good-quality baking chocolate for the smoothest melt. Chocolate chips may hold their shape and be thicker when melted.

Extract

Use peppermint extract, not mint extract, and add only a little. It is strong.

Tips for Success

  • Melt the chocolate gently and avoid getting any water in it, which makes it seize.
  • Let the dark layer firm up before spreading the white chocolate, so the layers stay separate.
  • Crush candy canes in a zip bag with a rolling pin.

Variations

  • Reverse the layers or use milk chocolate.
  • Add a few drops of food coloring to the white chocolate or sprinkle with sea salt.

Storage and Make-Ahead

Store in an airtight container in a cool place or the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. Keep it away from heat and humidity, which can soften the candy canes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my chocolate seize?

Water got into it, or it was overheated. Melt gently and keep utensils dry.

Can I use chips?

Yes, but baking bars melt more smoothly.

How do I get clean pieces?

Let it set fully and break it by hand, or cut it with a warm knife.
